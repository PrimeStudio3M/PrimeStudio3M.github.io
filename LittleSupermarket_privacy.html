Privacy Policy

App: Little Supermarket: Kids Games
Developer: PrimeStudio 3M
Last updated: September 21, 2026

Summary: Little Supermarket is a children's game with no advertising, no game accounts, and no behavioral tracking. Game progress is saved on your device. The optional Full Store purchase is protected by a parental gate and processed by Apple or Google. Store services and voluntary support emails are described below.

Information We Collect

Little Supermarket: Kids Games is published by PrimeStudio 3M and designed for young children. The game does not ask children to provide personal information and does not send their gameplay progress to our servers.

The game does not request or collect a child's name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or voice recordings. It has no account registration, chat, social features, or user uploads. We do not use advertising identifiers to track users or create behavioral profiles.

If a parent or guardian chooses to email us for support, we receive the sender's email address and the information included in the message. We use this information to answer the request and resolve the issue, not for advertising. Please do not send personal information about a child.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves progress, collected rewards, decorations, language and sound settings, and purchase-unlock status locally so that play can continue between sessions. We do not operate a cloud-save service or hold a server copy of this progress.

Parents can reset gameplay progress in the parents' section. Resetting progress does not cancel a purchase or necessarily remove all settings. Local app data can also be removed through device settings or by deleting the app. Device backups may retain or restore app data according to your Apple or Google account settings.

In-App Purchases

The app offers an optional, one-time Full Store purchase that unlocks additional content. Purchase actions in the app are protected by a parental gate. There are no subscriptions.

Payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The app uses Unity In-App Purchasing to communicate with the store, obtain product prices, check ownership, complete purchases, and restore access to purchased content. This involves purchase-related information such as product identifiers, transaction identifiers, receipts or purchase tokens, and ownership status. We do not receive your payment-card details or store-account password.

Gameplay is available offline. Store connections, purchases, and restoring purchases require an internet connection. Apple and Google process store-account and transaction information under their own privacy policies and may keep purchase records after the app is deleted.

Third-Party Services

The app is built with Unity and uses Unity In-App Purchasing for store functionality. We do not enable third-party advertising or gameplay analytics services. The store and platform services may process technical information needed to deliver their services.

More information is available in the providers' policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy

External links in the app's parents' section are protected by a parental gate. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages; visiting it opens a website outside the game. GitHub may process standard website-access information, such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page contains no added analytics scripts, advertising, or cookies.

Children's Privacy

We take children's privacy seriously. The game is designed for children to play without providing personal information. Purchase and external-link access is intended for parents and guardians. A parental gate helps restrict access to adult functions; it is not consent to collect a child's personal information.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so that we can investigate and remove it as appropriate.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Information needed for purchases is processed by the services described above. Support correspondence is processed through our email provider. We may disclose information if required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

Retention and Your Choices

Local progress remains on your device until it is reset or removed, subject to your device's backup settings. Support correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed to address the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request access to or deletion of personal information you have provided to us. Store-account data and purchase-record requests should be directed to Apple or Google.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, changes will be reflected on this page and the date above will be revised. If a change requires additional notice or consent, we will provide it as required.
