Privacy Policy — Parkour Run: Obstacle Adventure

Effective date: September 19, 2026
Developer: PrimeStudio 3M (Maksym Mykhailenko)
Contact: playprimeaxe@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you play Parkour Run: Obstacle Adventure (the “Game”) downloaded from the Amazon Appstore on Fire tablets and Fire TV devices, who processes it, and what choices you have. “We”, “us” and “our” refer to the developer named above.

In short:

  • The Game has no accounts, no chat, no online multiplayer and no user-generated content. Opponents in races are computer-controlled.
  • Your game progress, settings and purchased items are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that collect your personal data, and this version of the Game contains no analytics or attribution SDKs.
  • The Game shows ads provided by third-party advertising networks through AppLovin MAX. These partners may collect your device’s advertising identifier and technical device data to deliver and measure ads.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Amazon. We never receive your payment card details.
  • The Game is not directed to children under 16.
  • You can ask us to delete data at any time — see Data deletion.

1. Information we process ourselves

Game data on your device. Your progress, coins, gems, unlocked items, settings and the list of purchased items are saved locally on your device. This data never leaves your device through us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data in the device settings.

Emails you send us. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use them only to answer you and delete them when they are no longer needed for that purpose.

We do not collect your name, email address (unless you write to us), phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or camera input, or precise location.

2. In-app purchases (Amazon)

Purchases of in-game currency, bundles, cosmetic items and ad removal are processed by Amazon through the Amazon Appstore In-App Purchasing service. Amazon handles the payment; we never see your payment card or billing details.

To deliver and restore your purchases, the Game receives from Amazon: the product identifier, a purchase receipt identifier, the marketplace/country of the purchase and an app-specific Amazon user identifier. This information is used only to give you the item you paid for and to restore non-consumable purchases (for example “No Ads”) after reinstalling. It is stored on your device.

Amazon’s processing of your data is governed by the Amazon Privacy Notice. Parents can require a PIN or approval for purchases in the Amazon parental control settings of the device.

3. Advertising

The Game is free to play and shows advertisements: optional rewarded videos (you choose to watch them for an in-game bonus), full-screen ads at natural breaks between rounds, and banner ads on tablets. Purchasing “No Ads” removes full-screen and banner ads; optional rewarded videos remain available.

Ads are delivered through the AppLovin MAX mediation platform and the advertising partners listed below. When an ad is requested or shown, these partners may automatically collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (on Fire devices — the Amazon Advertising ID) and your “interest-based ads” setting;
  • device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type, the Game’s identifier and version;
  • your IP address, which can indicate an approximate location (city/region level);
  • ad interaction data: which ads were shown, clicked or completed.

They use this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where you have allowed it, show ads based on your interests. Each partner processes this data under its own privacy policy:

4. Game engine

The Game is built with the Unity engine. Unity may process limited technical information about your device (such as device model, operating system and crash information) as described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

5. Notifications

On tablets the Game may schedule local reminder notifications. They are created on your device and do not send any information to us. You can turn them off at any time in your device’s notification settings.

6. Your choices

  • Interest-based ads. You can turn off interest-based ads for your device: on Fire tablets in Settings → Privacy → Interest-Based Ads; on Fire TV in Settings → Preferences → Privacy Settings → Interest-based Ads. Ad partners must respect this setting. You will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.
  • Consent. Where a consent dialog was shown, you can change your choice by clearing the Game’s data (which resets the choice) and launching the Game again.
  • Remove ads. The “No Ads” purchase removes full-screen and banner ads.
  • Delete local data. Uninstall the Game or clear its data in the device settings.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). On Fire devices, Amazon disables the advertising identifier in child profiles (Amazon Kids), so ad partners cannot use it for interest-based advertising there. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

  • Consent — personalized advertising and access to the advertising identifier where consent is required.
  • Performance of a contract — delivering and restoring items you purchase.
  • Legitimate interests — showing non-personalized ads to keep the Game free, preventing ad fraud, and answering your emails.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

California and other US states. We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising partners’ use of your advertising identifier and device data for ads may be considered “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can opt out by turning off interest-based ads in your device settings (see Section 6) or by contacting us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Because we do not keep your personal data on our own servers, most requests about advertising data are fulfilled by the advertising partners. We will help you and forward your request where needed.

10. Data deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time, without creating any account:

  • Data on your device (progress, settings, purchase records): uninstall the Game or clear its data in the device settings. This deletes it immediately. Paid non-consumable items can still be restored later from your Amazon account.
  • Data held by advertising partners: turn off interest-based ads (Section 6) and use the privacy request options in the partners’ policies listed in Section 3, or email us and we will assist.
  • Emails you sent us: email playprimeaxe@gmail.com with the subject “Parkour Run data deletion”.

We respond to requests within 30 days.

11. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. We keep emails only as long as needed to answer you. Advertising partners and Amazon retain data according to their own policies. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

12. International transfers

Our advertising partners and Amazon may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. They are responsible for providing appropriate safeguards for such transfers as described in their policies.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. If a change significantly affects how data is processed, we will highlight it in the Game or in the store listing.
