Privacy Policy — Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Developer: PrimeStudio 3M · Contact: playprimeaxe@gmail.com

In short: Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle never asks a child for a name, e-mail, photo, contacts or location. There are no accounts, no chat and no sharing. Progress stays on the device. On Android, versions with ads show child-directed, non-personalized ads; on iPhone and iPad, Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle shows no ads. Purchases and outside links are behind a parental gate. Below we explain exactly which services receive technical data and why.

1. Which app this policy covers

This policy applies to Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle, a game for children developed by PrimeStudio 3M. Not every version has every feature below. Depending on the platform and version, the game may contain:

  • child-directed, non-personalized ads in the free Android version (section 4); Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle shows no ads on iPhone and iPad;
  • an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the rest of the game (section 5);
  • a microphone feature for a grown-up’s own voice recordings that stay on the device (section 7).

The store page for Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle (Data safety on Google Play, App Privacy on the App Store) shows exactly which of these the game uses. Versions published in other Android stores (for example Galaxy Store or Amazon Appstore) have all content unlocked and no in-app purchases; they may show the same child-directed ads as described in section 4.

2. What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts, precise location or any other information that identifies a child. Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no social features and no way to share content with other people. We do not use the Android advertising ID (the game removes the AD_ID permission) and we do not use Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA) or ask for tracking permission. We do not sell personal information. We do not run servers that receive data from Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle.

3. What stays on the device

Game progress, stickers, settings, purchases already made, and anything a child creates in Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle (for example, their own levels or drawings) are saved only on the device. They are deleted when the app is removed or when a grown-up resets progress in the parents’ area.

4. Advertising (Android only)

In versions of Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle with ads, the free Android version shows full-screen ads only at natural pauses — after a finished level, round or visit — never during play. There are no banner ads and no rewarded videos. Every ad request is marked as directed at children: “tag for child-directed treatment” (COPPA), under-age-of-consent treatment (GDPR), maximum ad content rating G, and non-personalized ads only. When Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle uses other ad networks through Google AdMob mediation (Unity Ads, Chartboost), the same child and non-consent settings are passed to them. Where the game offers an optional purchase, its store page says whether the purchase also removes ads.

To deliver and count an ad and to prevent fraud, the ad networks may process technical information such as the IP address (which can indicate an approximate location such as the country), device type and operating system, app version, interactions with the ad, diagnostics, and an app-scoped identifier used for frequency capping and fraud prevention. They may not use it to build a profile of the child or to show personalized ads. Their policies:

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Ads (only in versions that use AdMob mediation) — unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Chartboost (only in versions that use AdMob mediation) — chartboost.com/legal/privacy-policy

5. Purchases

Optional purchases are one-time (no subscriptions) and are paid through Google Play or the App Store. We never see payment details. The purchase screen and “restore purchases” are only in the parents’ area behind a parental gate.

Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle uses Unity Technologies’ purchasing library to talk to the store. In its current versions, when a grown-up starts, completes or restores a purchase, this library sends Unity a record of that purchase (product, price, currency, order or receipt ID) together with technical device information (for example device model, operating system version, language, memory size and install time; on iPhone and iPad also the identifier for vendor, IDFV). Unity uses it to process purchases and for purchase analytics; it is not used for advertising in Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle. Versions of the game built with an older version of this library do not send this record. See Unity’s privacy policy.

6. Crash and engine diagnostics

Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle is made with the Unity engine. Depending on the version, Unity’s engine diagnostics may be switched on: if the game crashes or has a technical problem, Unity may receive a technical report (device model, operating system, app and engine version, crash details). These reports do not contain names or contact details. We do not use any other analytics or tracking tools in Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle.

7. Microphone (only in versions with voice recording)

If voice recording is available in Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle, it lets a grown-up record their own voice, for example to read a story aloud. The microphone is used only when a grown-up chooses to record. Recordings are saved only on the device, are never uploaded or sent to us or anyone else, and are deleted from the app’s settings or when the app is removed.

8. Outside links and parental gate

Links to this policy, to our other games in the same app store and to support e-mail are only in the parents’ area, behind a parental gate that a young child cannot pass. A version of Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle installed from one store never sends you to a different store.

9. Data retention

We keep no data on servers. E-mails you send us are kept only while we answer them and are deleted 12 months after the question is resolved. Data processed by Google, Unity and Chartboost under sections 4–6 is kept according to their own policies linked above; we do not receive it in a form that identifies a child.

10. Children, parents and your rights

Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle is directed at children. We follow the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR rules for children, the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, the Google Play Families Policy and Apple’s App Store rules for the Kids Category. A parent or guardian can contact us at playprimeaxe@gmail.com with any question or request, including a request to review or delete information; we will answer and help pass the request to Google, Unity or Chartboost where they hold the data. You can also stop all data processing by deleting the app.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If a change is significant, we will update the store pages for Knotbay: Kids Rope Puzzle as well.
